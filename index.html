SLICE64-FM EDITOR
FM-1: not connected

Connect

Plug the FM-1 in USB, running the SLICE64-FM firmware (s1.3 or later). Chrome or Edge, then allow MIDI SysEx access.

Transfer

Idle.

A song sent is stored in the FM-1's flash and stays after power-off. Samples go to their own flash bank (about 8 s at 32 kHz, 16 s at 16 kHz).

FM-1 live Connect the FM-1 (Device tab) to see its screen here.

    
    
The FM-1's own screen and lights, as they change. With firmware s1.6 the panel plays the FM-1 too: click or touch the buttons and keys (several fingers at once, slide across the keys), drag or scroll the knobs. Right-click or Shift-click a button to hold it (HOME + OCT, SEQ + PRESETS with a mouse); click it again to let go. MASTER stays on the device.

    
Keyboard (as in the Windows emulator): arrows move · Space PLAY · Enter REC · Esc HOME · Shift SEL · F1–F5 HOME EDIT GLO FX SEQ · Delete ARP · Backspace note off · Tab PRESETS · W / X OCT− / OCT+ · PgUp/PgDn K1 · Home/End K2 · + / − K3 · * / / K4 · the two letter rows A Z E R… / Q S D F… and the number row play the keys.

Keys (grid focused)

      
Arrows move, Tab next track, Page Up/Down 16 rows.

      
Note column: the computer keyboard is a piano, two rows (physical positions, QWERTY or AZERTY):

        bottom row from Z/W = octave, top row from Q/A = octave + 1.

        ` or = note off.

      
Instrument / parameter: hex digits. FX: 0 1 2 3 4 C F R S.

      
Delete clears, Insert/Backspace push down / pull up the track.
